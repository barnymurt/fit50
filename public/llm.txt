# FIT50 — The 50-Day Challenge

> A 50-day behaviour challenge built around nine daily rules, with habit tracking, a workout programme, macro and hydration logging, and a printable completion certificate. Free to start; a one-time payment unlocks cloud sync, streak protection and data export.

FIT50 runs for fifty days. You keep a daily streak across nine rules, complete a short workout each day, log food and water, and finish with a certificate showing what you actually completed.

## The nine rules

Each rule is one concrete daily action. Tapping one is binary — done or not done. Missing a day breaks the streak unless you spend a streak-protection pass.

1. Feed Your Brain — Read 5 books in 50 days, or give 30 minutes a day on a project you've been putting off.
2. Move Your Body — Complete one workout from the four lines (A, B, C, D) every day.
3. Fuel Right — Track your macros every day. Hit your protein, carb and fat targets.
4. Crispy Clarity — No alcohol for 50 days.
5. Fresh Lungs — No smoking, no vaping. Find your nicotine by healthier means for 50 days.
6. Open Mind — Meditate for 10 minutes every day. Sit, breathe, notice.
7. Step It Up — Walk 10,000 steps every day.
8. Wet The Lips — Drink 2.5 litres of water every day.
9. Chill Out — End every shower cold, building toward a fully cold shower by day 50.
