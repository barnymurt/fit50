Library section background extends through the whole section

The lavender (quit list) / coral (drinks) / cream (meditation) page background used to be only on the body and the marquee strip. The .library section itself was unstyled so the region sections, the tile grid, and the empty state had a different visual feel from the filter bar above them. The user asked for the page colour to fill the whole section, matching the marquee.

src/styles/on-the-house.css: .library now picks up var(--on-the-house-bg) so the page colour runs from the top of the filter bar, through the region sections, all the way to the bottom of the empty state. Tiles (.tile) are at 0.92 alpha on top of the field, so they read as near-white cards floating on the colour.
