Quit list: switch page tone from lavender to cream

src/app/account/quit-list/page.tsx

The quit-list and drinks pages shared lavender (#e4def3) as their
overall background tone, which made them feel like duplicates when
navigated between. Switched quit-list to cream (#F2D9A2) so the three
sub-pages now have three distinct tones: drinks=lavender,
meditation=teal, quit-list=cream.

Changes:
- Added --ql-cream: #F2D9A2 token next to the existing --ql-lavender
  tokens (kept lavender-* in place; modal notes still use
  --ql-lavender-soft for their soft callout backgrounds).
- .ql-root base background: lavender -> cream.
- .ql-marquee background: lavender -> cream (carries ink text on
  cream, design system allows).
- .ql-filter-wrap (sticky filter bar) background: lavender -> cream.

Tiles, pills, empty state, and modal remain on paper so they continue
to pop against the cream backdrop. Filter pills keep their 1px border
to stay legible.

Build clean. /account/quit-list unchanged size.
