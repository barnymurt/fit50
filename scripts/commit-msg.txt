Meditation (Ten Minutes) page now uses teal

The user wanted the Ten Minutes page in the teal tone from the design system instead of cream. Renamed .theme-cozy to .theme-teal and rewrote every rule so the page colour is teal (#4A9B9B) with paper text (#FBF7EE) for contrast.

src/styles/on-the-house.css:
  - --on-the-house-bg: var(--teal) and --on-the-house-fg: var(--paper)
  - every text rule (eyebrow, region-title, region-blurb, count, count-meta, langs, org, blurb, etc.) inverted to paper because teal is dark
  - tiles stay paper with coral-on-teal hover behaviour
  - pills: glassy (rgba(255,255,255,0.18) background, paper border) and invert to paper/ink-deep when pressed
  - .modal-title / .modal-eyebrow / .modal-section-title / .modal-meta > div > strong all paper
  - .modal-note is glassy at 0.18 alpha, paper text
  - .modal-action-primary is paper bg with teal text, hover goes to alpha 0.85
  - .modal-close is paper bg with teal text, hover inverts to ink-deep
  - .nav a, .footer-link, .coda a all paper; hover dials to 0.7 alpha so they don't fight the field

src/app/account/meditation/page.tsx: page root switched from theme-cozy to theme-teal.

So the three sub-pages now read as:
  - Quit list: lavender (peaceful)
  - Drinks: coral (vibrant, brand orange)
  - Meditation: teal (cool, calming, the design system's 'tranquil' tone)
