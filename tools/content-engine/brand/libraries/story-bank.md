# Origin story bank

Eight episodes from the founder's first-person arc. The content
engine draws from this bank; nothing in the pillar `origin-story`
is invented. First person as Barny, self-deprecating, specific,
a bit daft. This is the **only** pillar that may mention 75 Hard.

Each episode is a moment, not a moral. The renderers turn the
moment into slides; the moment itself is the truth.

---

## Episode 1 — The Woolies basket

I did my first shop after the rule set in. Two litres of water.
A tub of Greek yoghurt. A bag of oats. Six bananas. And a
four-pack of Arnott's biscuits, which I bought like an idiot,
because the rule set said "Fuel Right" and the four-pack
was on special.

I made it halfway to the car before I ate the first one.

---

## Episode 2 — Like an idiot, I signed up for an ultra

Two weeks into my second 50-day stretch I entered a backyard
ultra. A backyard ultra is a thing where you run a 6.7 km
loop every hour, on the hour, until you can't finish one in
the time. The winner is the last person standing.

I finished my 50 days. I did not finish the ultra. I made it
to loop six before my calves told me to stop. The first
person I told about it said "well at least the 50 days
stuck" and I said "yeah, that's the whole point" and we
both laughed.

## Episode 3 — The caneca

I grew up in a pub family. Three beers later was a real place.
When I was working out the price of FIT50 Premium I kept
coming back to one number: the price of a caneca. That's a
large beer in the local dialect. €5.99 is the price of a
caneca.

It's the only price I'm comfortable charging. The toolkit
should cost what a night out would cost. If the toolkit is
worth more than that, I haven't built it right.

---

## Episode 4 — 75 Hard in transit

I did 75 Hard in 2020. I was on a plane every other week for
work. The diet was the easy part. The two workouts a day,
one of which had to be outside, were the hard part. I did a
lot of hotel car-park lunges. I did a lot of 4am gym sessions
in cities I couldn't name in daylight.

I finished. I learned that I could finish. I also learned
that finishing something because you told yourself you would
is a different muscle from finishing something because the
rules said so.

---

## Episode 5 — Three beers later, day 1

Day 1 of my first FIT50, I had a friend's birthday the night
before. Three beers, late night, the head on me the next
morning was real. I sat down with the nine rules and the
calculator. I drank the two litres of water before lunch.
I did a 20-minute walk. I went to bed at 9pm.

It was the most boring day I've had in years. It was also
the most useful.

---

## Episode 6 — The day I missed and used the banana

Day 18 of my second stretch. I was on a flight, missed the
workout, missed the water, missed the books. Streak about
to break. Premium user. Used the streak protection.

The day I broke it I also had the calmest day in weeks. I
went to bed knowing the streak was safe and that I had one
charm left in the next 25 days. The tool is supposed to
take friction out of getting back to the rules. It did.

---

## Episode 7 — Buddy pair, Saturday morning

A mate bought a pair. Saturday morning, both of us tapped
"Start today" at the same time. We compared streaks every
evening for a week. He made it to day 23 before a work trip
broke him. I made it to day 50.

The point isn't that he didn't finish. The point is that
neither of us did it alone. The pair is the whole feature.

---

## Episode 8 — The bookshelf, the projector, the prescription

My bookshelf used to be a shrine to unread books. I bought
them. I didn't read them. Feed Your Brain made me read them
because the rule was on the screen every morning. The
projector is a habit, not a dream. The prescription is a
habit, not a thought. Read the book. Drink the water. Step
the step. The rules are the rules.
