01 · Palette

Six colors, one rule line

Ink and paper carry the page. Coral is the only action color. Teal, cream and lavender belong to whole sections, never to small details.

Ink

#1A1A1A · ink

All body copy and headings. Primary button fill on light. Workouts section ground.

Paper

#FAF6EE · paper

Default page background. Becomes the text color on ink and teal grounds.

Coral

#E88B5A · coral

The accent. Completed habit tiles, progress fills, focus rings, hover states, marquee type, text selection.

Teal

#4A9B9B · teal

Rules section ground and the streak card. Also means "success": a day with 7+ habits done, a finished timer.

Cream

#F2D9A2 · cream

Partial progress in the 50-day grid, and the streak numeral on teal. A soft tone, never a button.

Lavender

#D8B8D0 · lavender

Calculator section ground, and the far stop of the hero gradient orb. Carries ink text, not paper.

White

#FFFFFF · white

Panel color above paper: the tracker card, the FAQ section. Distinct from paper — do not swap them.

Rule

rgba(26,26,26,0.12) · rule

Every divider and panel border on light. On dark, use rgba(254,254,254,0.14) (rule-light) or paper at 15%.

Opacity ladder

The codebase tints ink and paper rather than adding greys. Stick to these steps.

100% body

85% hover

70% prose

50% labels

30% borders

12% rules

03 · Typography

Three typefaces, strict jobs

Display · font-display

Fraunces

Weights 400–700, always set at 400. Every heading, every big numeral, the FIT50 wordmark. Serif fallback: Georgia.

Body · font-body

Inter

Weights 400/500/600. Prose, labels, buttons, inputs, all caption text. Features cv11 and ss01 are on globally.

Marquee · font-marquee

Lilita One

One job only: the scrolling section headers. Never used for UI text, never below 5rem.

04 · Space & grid

One container, twelve columns

Container

max-width: 1280px · 0 auto

Gutters 24px on mobile, 40px from md up. Full-bleed grounds, contained content — always.

Section rhythm

clamp(5rem, 10vw, 8rem)

The section spacing token, used as bottom padding. Sections with a marquee header pad the top to 160px / 224px instead.

Gaps

24px cards · 32px layout · 6px grid · 1px cube

Headline-to-content is 48px, growing to 64px at md.

Column patterns in use

7 · headline block
4 · streak
8 · cube + 50 days
6 offset · calculator panel

Everything collapses to a single column below md. The tracker splits at lg, not md.

05 · Shape, motion, focus

Square by default

Radius

0px — panels, cards, inputs, tiles, grid cells.

Full — the default Button, and the 32px accordion toggle. Nothing in between.

Easing & duration

cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)

  • 200ms — buttons, links, hover color
  • 300ms — habit tiles, nav, accordion body
  • 500ms — progress bar, solved-cube glow
  • 700ms — rule card flip (rotateY 180°)
  • 60s / 200–240s — marquee loops

All of it collapses to 0.01ms under prefers-reduced-motion.

Focus & elevation

Focus is always a 2px coral outline at 3px offset with a 2px radius. There are no drop shadows anywhere in the system — the one exception is the solved cube's 0 0 0 4px rgba(232,139,90,0.35) ring. Depth comes from ground color and 1px rules.

06 · Usage

Do / don't

Do

  • Use coral for exactly one thing per view: the thing to act on.
  • Tint ink and paper for hierarchy instead of reaching for grey.
  • Set headings in Fraunces 400 — never bold them.
  • Keep captions uppercase with 0.12em tracking, always 12px.
  • Change ground color to signal a new section.
  • Let 1px rules do the dividing work.

Don't

  • Round a card, panel or input — squares are the house style.
  • Add drop shadows to build depth.
  • Use Lilita One anywhere except a full-width marquee.
  • Put paper text on cream or lavender; both carry ink.
  • Introduce a new accent hue. Coral is the accent.
  • Swap white for paper in panels — the two are distinct.
